About this version

Trade Journal Mobile v5.2.0 — the phone companion to Trade Journal Desktop v5.2.0 (Windows & Mac).

Same rules as desktop: Jadu Tona only, XAUUSD, $1 = 10 pips, $1 per pip per 1.0 lot. Goat Funded Trader $5K Two-Step GOAT rules on the Dashboard.

What mobile does not do (yet): no screenshot OCR, no offline Whisper voice, no MT5/Excel/PDF/Word import, no live sync with your PC. Your phone journal and your PC journal are separate copies — export CSV from one and import it into the other to move trades across.

Your data never leaves this phone. There is no account and no server.
